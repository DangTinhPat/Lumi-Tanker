# B4: stronger wheel request and capture priority during posture transfer

## Robot evidence before this change

Branch: `v3.6`. Log: `/home/dvt/tap_logs/tap_20261018_201044.log`.
One live StandUp trial, starting at 31.591 s; floor infeasibility fault at
36.149 s. Repeated Fault snapshots are not new trials. No Balancing entry.
Positive pitch is nose down; body +X is forward. Hip Jacobian is evaluated
in native joint coordinates; motor mapping handles side signs.

At B4 0.831–1.077 s the wheel request repeatedly saturates at -16 rad/s,
while encoders reach approximately -18 to -19 rad/s. At 0.996 s the COM
lean is -0.058 rad, capture point -0.093 rad, and posture requests hip
-0.454 rad/s. With the measured positive hip Jacobian, this hip contribution
moves COM farther backward. Capture is subsequently lost. Floor clearance
falls from 16.6 mm at 0.996 s to 14.83 mm at 1.077 s; predicted clearance
is then 9.06 mm. The previous shortened rolling handoff never becomes
eligible (`rolling_gate=0`), so this log does not establish its effectiveness.

## Changes

- Tuned wheel speed request cap: 16 → 20 rad/s.
- Tuned wheel acceleration request cap: 4 → 5 m/s².
- Retain wheel entry blend 60 ms, hip blend 80 ms, hip speed cap 2 rad/s
  and hip acceleration cap 12 rad/s². No motor torque/current register change.
- Add opt-in `catch_posture_capture_guard`, enabled in tuned recovery.
  Only during B4 posture transfer, if COM and capture point are on the same
  side and both posture and total requested hip rate move COM outward
  according to the measured Jacobian, fade that total rate toward zero.
  Fade starts at |COM lean|=0.015 rad and completes at 0.030 rad.
  Initial lift and Support do not use this projection. Downstream hip
  acceleration, travel, tracking and floor constraints still apply. This is
  a local command projection, not a guarantee of physical COM motion.
- SC telemetry adds `post_guard`; `feedback_rate` is after this projection,
  `level_rate` remains the raw posture contribution and `applied_rate` reports
  the rate after downstream constraints.

Coupling: wheel radius 0.04 m makes the integral speed bound increase from
0.64 to 0.80 m/s and the rolling handoff speed bound from 0.79 to 0.95 m/s.
Body, COM, tracking and floor gates remain active. LQR retains its own caps.
Higher speed requests alone do not guarantee shorter retreat.

Preserved: fold 2.05 rad, fixed knees, skipped B3, body COM offset +5 mm,
floor/fall/stale guards. No new sensors or simulator state dependencies.

Runtime rollback in Idle/Fault (overrides are lost on serial reset):

```text
SET standwheel 16
SET standaccel 4
SET standpostguard 0
```
